# IT Kamianets

A digital ecosystem for the IT community of Kamianets-Podilskyi.
The goal is to create a real, scalable, production-ready mobile-first platform.

---

# 🎯 Mission

Build the digital operating system of IT Kamianets.

The platform connects:

- People
- Education
- Businesses
- Jobs
- Events
- Competitions
- Projects
- Services
- Community
- Merch

---

# 🏗 Architecture Overview

The platform is divided into independent feature domains.

Each domain:

- Owns its backend logic
- Owns its frontend pages
- Defines its data models
- Integrates via shared contracts

All teams follow shared standards:

- UI design system
- API structure
- Role & permission model
- Definition of done

---

# 📦 Feature Domains

---

## 1. People

Public community members.

Includes:

- Public profile pages
- Roles (student, mentor, founder, etc.)
- Achievements
- Linked projects
- Event participation history

Purpose:
Create identity and recognition inside the ecosystem.

---

## 2. Team

Internal IT Kamianets organization.

Includes:

- Core team members
- Developers building the platform
- Mentors
- Organizational structure
- Contact information

Purpose:
Represent the structure behind the community.

---

## 3. Events

Community gatherings and learning activities.

Includes:

- Meetups
- Workshops
- Conferences
- Registration system
- QR check-in
- Feedback

Purpose:
Drive engagement and offline interaction.

---

## 4. Competitions

Hackathons, tournaments, and challenges.

Includes:

- Competition pages
- Team formation
- Submissions
- Voting
- Leaderboards
- Prize tracking

Purpose:
Encourage innovation and collaboration.

---

## 5. Jobs

Career opportunities for the community.

Includes:

- Job listings
- Internship listings
- Company pages
- Application flow
- Status tracking

Purpose:
Connect talent with companies.

---

## 6. Businesses

Local IT companies and digital agencies.

Includes:

- Company profiles
- Services offered
- Tech stack
- Reviews
- Contact details

Purpose:
Map the local economy.

---

## 7. Schools

Education centers and training programs.

Includes:

- Courses
- Teachers
- Programs
- Enrollment system
- Certificates

Purpose:
Support continuous learning.

---

## 8. Projects

Showcase of student and local projects.

Includes:

- Product pages
- Team members
- GitHub links
- Media
- Likes and feedback

Purpose:
Build real portfolios.

---

## 9. Services

Freelancers and service providers.

Includes:

- Developer profiles
- Design services
- Consulting offers
- Contact or booking flow

Purpose:
Enable real economic activity inside the ecosystem.

---

## 10. Shop / Merch

Community merchandise.

Includes:

- T-shirts
- Hoodies
- Stickers
- Event merchandise
- Limited releases

Purpose:
Support sustainability and brand identity.

---

## 11. Feed

Central communication layer.

Includes:

- Announcements
- Event reminders
- Job highlights
- Community posts
- Media content
- Comments and reactions

Purpose:
Connect all domains together.
